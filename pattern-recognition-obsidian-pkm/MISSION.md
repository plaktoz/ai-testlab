# Mission: Pattern Recognition & Obsidian PKM

## Why
Most people accumulate notes but never build the cognitive library that lets experts see structure where others see noise. This course exists to change that: by combining the science of deliberate practice with vault architecture designed for connection, the learner builds both a trained mind and a system that compounds pattern recognition over time. The outcome is faster, more reliable insight across unfamiliar domains.

## Success looks like
- Applies deliberate practice principles to any skill domain to accelerate pattern acquisition, not just accumulate rote repetitions
- Maintains a mental model library of cross-domain frameworks (First Principles, Second-Order Thinking, Munger's 80-90) ready to deploy when encountering novel situations
- Operates an Obsidian vault built on Zettelkasten/Evergreen principles — atomic, densely linked, topology-first — that surfaces emergent patterns through navigation rather than recall
- Runs a daily PKM loop (CODE → PARA → SRS review) that turns passive note accumulation into active pattern reinforcement
- Can derive structural patterns from first principles in unfamiliar domains rather than borrowing someone else's analogy

## Constraints
- Learning from research papers and structured sources — no access to proprietary systems
- Self-paced, one lesson at a time
- Obsidian-specific tooling recommendations (not other PKM apps)

## Out of scope
- Machine learning or AI-based pattern recognition systems
- Programming implementations of knowledge graphs
- Productivity systems not tied to knowledge management or pattern surfacing
