# Mission: Loop Engineering

## Why
Loops are the fundamental mechanism by which intelligent systems — human or artificial — operate in uncertain environments. Understanding loop engineering lets you design AI agents that improve through iteration rather than relying on raw single-pass capability, and gives you the vocabulary to choose the right architecture, set the right oversight model, and debug failures systematically when things go wrong.

## Success looks like
- Explain the OODA loop structure and articulate why cycling tempo matters more than raw capability
- Place a system on the human-in-the-loop spectrum and justify the oversight level for a given action type
- Identify which agentic loop pattern (reflection, planning, tool use, orchestration, eval-optimize) fits a given task
- Sketch a reflection or planning loop for a concrete task, including stopping conditions and checkpoints
- Name and categorize loop failure modes (planning, tool, efficiency) when given a system description
- Recognize meta-loop patterns (trajectory storage → fine-tuning) as second-order improvement mechanisms

## Constraints
- Learning from research papers and engineering blog posts — no access to proprietary systems
- Self-paced, one lesson at a time

## Out of scope
- Multi-agent coordination protocols beyond shared orchestrator patterns
- Fine-tuning pipelines beyond the trajectory-storage mechanism
- Production deployment, infrastructure, and scaling concerns
