# Mental Models Resources

## Knowledge

- [Farnam Street — Mental Models: The Best Way to Make Intelligent Decisions](https://fs.blog/mental-models/)
  The most comprehensive free resource on mental models. Maintained by Shane Parrish. Use for: canonical definitions, categorisation, and deep-dives on any single model.

- [James Clear — Mental Models: How to Train Your Brain to Think in New Ways](https://jamesclear.com/mental-models)
  Practical, beginner-friendly overview. Strong on psychology and business models. Use for: real-world application framing.

- [Book: _The Great Mental Models Vol. 1–4_ — Shane Parrish & Rhiannon Beaubien](https://fs.blog/tgmm/)
  The definitive book series on mental models, grounded in history and primary sources. Use for: deep understanding of any model covered in the course.

- [Book: _Thinking, Fast and Slow_ — Daniel Kahneman](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/11468377-thinking-fast-and-slow)
  Nobel Prize-winning psychology research on how humans make decisions. Use for: cognitive biases, loss aversion, anchoring, and all system-1/2 thinking models.

- [Book: _Poor Charlie's Almanack_ — Charlie Munger](https://www.poorcharliesalmanack.com/)
  Charlie Munger's lifelong case for mental model thinking. Use for: wisdom on how to build a latticework of models.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/mentalmodels](https://www.reddit.com/r/mentalmodels/)
  Community discussing mental models in practice. Good for: examples of how others apply models to real situations.

- [Farnam Street Learning Community](https://fs.blog/community/)
  High-signal community around Shane Parrish's work. Use for: peer discussion, book clubs, deeper application.
