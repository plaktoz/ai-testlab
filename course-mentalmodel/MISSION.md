# Mission: Top 50 Mental Models

## Why
To build a toolkit of thinking patterns that make everyday decisions — at work, home, and relationships — clearer and smarter. The goal is not academic knowledge but fluent, automatic application: reaching for the right mental model the moment a real situation calls for it.

## Success looks like
- Can name and explain the top 50 mental models in plain, simple language
- Can spot which model applies when facing a real decision or problem
- Can explain any model to someone else using 2–3 concrete examples from daily life
- Completes all 50 lessons and reference sheets, passing the quiz for each

## Constraints
- Explain everything at a 5-year-old level — no jargon, no abstract language
- Each model needs 2–3 real-life examples, not textbook ones
- Quiz after every concept until the user gets it right
- Sessions should be short and completable quickly

## Out of scope
- Deep academic theory (game theory proofs, statistical derivations)
- Business strategy frameworks beyond what a mental model naturally covers
- Philosophy of mind or epistemology for its own sake
