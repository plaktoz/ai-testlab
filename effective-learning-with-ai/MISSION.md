# Mission: Building Effective AI Tutoring Systems

## Why
To understand the cognitive science and technical architecture behind AI tutoring systems deeply enough to build or improve one — translating research into working systems that genuinely help learners retain and apply knowledge, not just retrieve it faster.

## Success looks like
- Explain the difference between a productivity assistant and a genuine tutoring system, and design accordingly
- Implement a student model that tracks proficiency, recent events, and forgetting curves
- Design a multi-agent tutoring architecture with a Diagnostician, Planner, and Tutor agent
- Choose the right knowledge tracing algorithm (Bayesian, neural, LLM-dynamic) for a given learning context
- Evaluate a tutoring system against real benchmarks (not just vibes)

## Constraints
- Learning from research papers — no access to proprietary codebases
- Target: personal or small-team systems (not enterprise-scale ITS)
- Time: self-paced, one lesson at a time

## Out of scope
- Robot tutoring systems (RTS) / social-physical embodied agents
- Learning management systems (LMS) / course authoring platforms
- Emotional intelligence / affective computing aspects (for now)
