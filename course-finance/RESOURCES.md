# Finance for Kids — Resources

## Knowledge

- [Jumpstart Coalition: National Standards in K-12 Personal Finance Education](https://www.jumpstart.org/what-we-do/support-financial-education/standards/)
  The gold-standard US framework for financial literacy by age. Use for: validating which concepts are developmentally appropriate for ages 4–6.

- [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: Money as You Grow (ages 3–5)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/money-as-you-grow/)
  CFPB's age-appropriate money milestones. Use for: grounding lessons in evidence-based child development research.

- [Council for Economic Education: Financial Fitness for Life (grades K–2)](https://www.econedlink.org/resources/?grades=k-2&topics=personal-finance)
  Teacher-vetted lesson plans for young children. Use for: activity ideas and vocabulary scaffolding.

- [MoneySmart for Young People (FDIC)](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/consumers/money-smart/teach-money-smart/money-smart-for-young-people/)
  Free FDIC curriculum for Pre-K through Grade 2. Use for: coin recognition activities, earning/saving frameworks.

- [PBS Kids: For Parents — Teaching Kids About Money](https://www.pbs.org/parents/thrive/how-to-teach-kids-about-money-at-every-age)
  Developmental guidance by age. Use for: calibrating language complexity and concept scope for age 5.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/personalfinance — Wiki: Teaching kids about money](https://www.reddit.com/r/personalfinance/wiki/index)
  Parent-tested strategies. Use for: real-world implementation ideas (allowance systems, chore charts).

- [Common Sense Media: Best Finance Apps and Games for Kids](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/lists/best-financial-literacy-apps-for-kids)
  Curated app reviews for children's financial literacy. Use for: supplemental interactive tools.
