🎯 Quiz Time!

Ready to show what you know? Click the best answer!

Q1 — What colour is a penny?
✅ Yes! Pennies are brown/copper coloured! They're easy to spot! 🟤
🙈 Pennies are brown / copper coloured — different from all the others!
Q2 — A dime is worth how many cents?
✅ Perfect! A dime = 10 cents! Remember: D for Dime, D for 10 (dix in French 😄)!
🙈 A dime is worth 10 cents. It's small but mighty!
Q3 — How many pennies make a quarter (25¢)?
✅ That's right! 25 pennies = 1 quarter! Great counting! 🎉
🙈 A quarter = 25 pennies! Count them: 1, 2, 3... all the way to 25!
Q4 — Which is worth MORE — a $1 bill or 3 quarters?
✅ Brilliant! $1 = 100 cents, but 3 quarters = only 75 cents. The bill wins! 💵
🙈 A $1 bill is worth 100 cents. Three quarters are only 75 cents — so the bill wins!
Q5 — Which coin is the SMALLEST in size but still worth more than a penny?
✅ Super smart! The dime is tiny but worth 10 cents! Size is tricky! 😄
🙈 The dime is the smallest coin, but it's worth 10 cents — more than a nickel!

🌟 Real-Life Challenge!

Ask a grown-up for some real coins. Sort them into four groups: Penny, Nickel, Dime, Quarter.

Then count how many cents you have in total. Can you say each coin's name out loud?

Bonus: Can you find something that costs less than 25 cents? 🛒
