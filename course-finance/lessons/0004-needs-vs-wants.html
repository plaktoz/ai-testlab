Needs vs Wants

Do I HAVE to have it, or do I just WANT it?

📖 Story Time

Zara had saved 20 coins! She went to the shop with her dad.

She saw SO many things she wanted: a toy dinosaur 🦕, a new colouring book 🎨, yummy biscuits 🍪, and some new shoes 👟.

Her dad said: "We only have 20 coins, Zara. Let's think — which things do you need, and which are things you just want?"

Zara stopped and thought hard... 🤔

💡 Needs and Wants

✅ A NEED

Something you must have to be healthy and safe.

Without it, you could get sick or hurt.

  • 🍎 Food
  • 💧 Water
  • 🏠 A home
  • 👕 Clothes
  • 💊 Medicine when sick

🌟 A WANT

Something nice to have — but you can live happily without it.

It makes life more fun, but you don't need it to be safe.

  • 🦕 Toy dinosaur
  • 🍩 Doughnuts
  • 🎮 Video games
  • 🎨 New colouring book
  • 🍦 Ice cream
💡 The BIG question to ask yourself:
"Would I get sick or hurt if I didn't have this?"
If YES → it's probably a need.
If NO → it's probably a want. 🤓

Trick question: Are new shoes a need or a want? 🤔
Basic shoes = a NEED (you need to protect your feet). But the fanciest shoes when you already have good ones = a WANT!

📚 Source: Council for Economic Education — Financial Literacy K–2

Q1 — Which one is a NEED?
✅ Yes! Food is a NEED — your body needs it every day to grow and be strong!
🙈 Food is the need! Your body must have food to be healthy!
Q2 — Which one is a WANT?
✅ Right! A toy dinosaur is a WANT — fun to have, but you don't need it to be safe!
🙈 The toy dinosaur is a want — it's lovely but not needed to stay healthy!
Q3 — Zara has 10 coins. She needs food (8 coins) and wants a toy (6 coins). What should she buy FIRST?
✅ Smart thinking! Always buy what you NEED first. Then see if you have coins left for wants!
🙈 Always buy needs first! Food is a need, so it comes before the toy!
Q4 — Is medicine a need or a want?
✅ Absolutely! Medicine is a NEED when you're sick — your body needs help to get better!
🙈 Medicine is a need when you're sick — it helps your body fight illness!
Q5 — The BEST question to ask about any item is:
✅ Perfect! Ask yourself: "Would I get sick or hurt without it?" That tells you if it's a need!
🙈 The smart question is: "Would I get sick or hurt without this?" That's how you spot a need!
