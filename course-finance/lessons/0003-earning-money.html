Earning Money

Work hard → earn coins → get things you want!

📖 Story Time

Zara the koala really, really wanted a new puzzle. It cost 10 coins.

She asked her mum: "Can I have 10 coins?"

Mum smiled and said: "I'll give you coins when you help around the house! That way, you earn them — just like I do at work!"

Zara thought about it... "So if I do chores, I get coins? And then I can buy my puzzle?!"

Mum nodded. Zara jumped up and got to work! 💨🧹

💡 What Does "Earning" Mean?

Earning means you do a job or help someone, and they give you money to say "thank you!"

💡 Earn
To get money by doing work or helping others.

Grown-ups earn money by going to work every day — like being a doctor, teacher, or baker. When you're young, you can earn money by helping at home — these are called chores!

🎯 Quiz Time!

Think carefully! Pick the best answer for each question.

Q1 — What does it mean to EARN money?
✅ That's right! Earning means doing work and getting paid for it! 💪
🙈 Earning means getting money by doing work — like chores or a job!
Q2 — What is a CHORE?
✅ Yes! A chore is a helpful job at home — like sweeping or making your bed!
🙈 A chore is a helpful job at home — like sweeping, tidying, or watering plants!
Q3 — Zara earns 2 coins each day. How many coins does she have after 3 days?
✅ Amazing maths! 2 + 2 + 2 = 6! You're brilliant! 🧮
🙈 2 coins × 3 days = 6 coins! Count with your fingers: 2, 4, 6!
Q4 — Why do grown-ups go to work?
✅ Exactly right! Grown-ups work to earn money so the family can buy food, clothes, and a home!
🙈 Grown-ups work to earn money for the family — to pay for food, clothes, and a home!
Q5 — Which is an example of EARNING money?
✅ Yes! Washing dishes + getting paid = earning! You did a job and got rewarded! 🙌
🙈 Earning is doing a job and getting paid — like washing dishes and receiving coins!
