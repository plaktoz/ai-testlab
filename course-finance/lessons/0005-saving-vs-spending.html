Saving vs Spending

Keep some, use some — the secret of the piggy bank!

📖 Story Time

Zara got 5 coins for her birthday from Grandma. 🎂

Her friend Leo spent ALL his birthday coins the same day — on sweets, a balloon, and stickers. Fun! But the next week, Leo had nothing left and couldn't buy the cool book he really wanted. 😢

Zara remembered what her mum said: "Save some, spend some — and you'll always have something for later!"

Zara put 3 coins in her piggy bank 🐷 and spent 2 coins on a yummy apple juice. 🧃

The next week, Zara used her saved coins PLUS new coins from chores to buy that book! 📚🎉

💡 Saving vs Spending

🐷

SAVING

Keeping money for later.
The more you save, the more you'll have for something BIG!

🛍️

SPENDING

Using money now to buy something.
Once you spend it, it's gone!

💡 The Golden Rule
Never spend ALL your money at once! Always save some — even a little bit — for later. 🌟

🐷 Meet the Piggy Bank!

A piggy bank is a safe place to keep your saved coins. Every time you put a coin in, your savings GROW — like a plant! 🌱

The longer you leave the coins in, the more coins you'll have when you need them!

🎯 Quiz Time!

You're doing great! Show me what you know!

Q1 — What does SAVING money mean?
✅ Yes! Saving means keeping money so you have it for something later! 🐷
🙈 Saving means keeping money for later — like putting it in a piggy bank!
Q2 — Leo spent ALL his coins on sweets. Next week he wanted a book but had no money. What was his mistake?
✅ Exactly right! Leo spent ALL his money and had none left for later. Always save some!
🙈 Leo's mistake was spending everything with no savings left for what he really wanted!
Q3 — Zara has 10 coins. She spends 4 coins on a snack. How many does she have left?
✅ Great maths! 10 − 4 = 6 coins left! Well done! 🧮
🙈 10 − 4 = 6 coins! When you spend, you take away from your total!
Q4 — What is a PIGGY BANK used for?
✅ Yes! A piggy bank keeps your saved coins safe so they grow over time! 🐷✨
🙈 A piggy bank is used to keep saved coins safely — so your money grows!
Q5 — The Golden Rule about spending is:
✅ The Golden Rule! Always save some — even just a little — for later. Smart! 🌟
🙈 The Golden Rule: always save some, don't spend it all! Keep a little back for later!
