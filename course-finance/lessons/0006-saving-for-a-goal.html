Saving for a Goal

Pick something you want, then save up to get it!

📖 Story Time

Zara saw a beautiful telescope at the shop. 🔭 She could see the moon through it!

The price tag said: 20 coins. That was a LOT! Zara only had 3 coins.

She could have cried. But instead, she said: "I'm going to save up for it!"

Every week, she put 4 coins away in her piggy bank. She put a picture of the telescope on the fridge so she would remember her goal. 📸

After 5 weeks — she had 20 coins plus her original 3! She bought the telescope AND had coins to spare! 🎉🔭

The waiting made it feel even more special when she got it.

💡 What is a Savings Goal?

A savings goal is something you decide you want to save up for. You pick the thing, find out the price, and make a plan to save enough coins to buy it.

💡 Three steps to reach a savings goal
Step 1: 🎯 Choose what you want and find out the price.
Step 2: 📅 Decide how many coins to save each week.
Step 3: 🐷 Keep saving until you get there — don't give up!

⏳ Waiting is a superpower!

When you wait and save, you feel so proud when you finally get the thing you wanted. It feels 10 times better than buying something on a whim! This is called delayed gratification — a very grown-up skill that even most adults find hard! 💪

📚 Source: PBS Parents — Teaching Kids About Money at Every Age

📅 Your own savings plan:

Think of something YOU want. How much does it cost? If you save 2 coins per week, how many weeks until you can buy it?

🧮 Formula: Weeks needed = Price ÷ Coins saved per week

Example: A $5 toy ÷ save $1 per week = 5 weeks!

🎯 Quiz Time!

Final stretch! You've learned so much. Show me! 🌟

Q1 — What is a savings GOAL?
✅ Yes! A savings goal is something you decide to save up to buy! 🎯
🙈 A savings goal is something you decide to save up to buy — like a toy or a book!
Q2 — A toy costs 12 coins. You save 3 coins a week. How many weeks to reach your goal?
✅ Brilliant! 12 ÷ 3 = 4 weeks! You're a maths superstar! 🧮
🙈 12 coins ÷ 3 coins per week = 4 weeks! Divide the price by what you save!
Q3 — What is the FIRST step to save for a goal?
✅ Exactly! First you pick your goal and find the price — then you can make a plan!
🙈 Step 1: choose what you want and find out the price — then you know how much to save!
Q4 — Zara has 8 coins saved for a 20-coin telescope. How many more does she need?
✅ Spot on! 20 − 8 = 12 more coins needed! 🧮🌟
🙈 20 − 8 = 12 coins more to go! Subtract what you have from the price!
Q5 — Why does waiting and saving feel SO good when you finally get the thing?
✅ YES! When you earn it yourself, it feels AMAZING — much better than just being given it! 💪🏆
🙈 It feels great because YOU worked hard and earned it — that pride is the best feeling!
