📖 Story Time — The Big Finish!

Zara's grandpa came to visit. He brought her a birthday gift — 30 coins! 😲

Zara remembered everything she had learned. She took three jars from the cupboard and put a sticker on each one:

  • 🛍️ SPEND — for things I want now
  • 🐷 SAVE — for something big later
  • ❤️ GIVE — to help others

She carefully put 10 coins in each jar. Her grandpa watched, then said:

"Zara, you are already smarter about money than most grown-ups I know." 🌟

💡 The Three-Jar System

When you get money — from chores, birthdays, or gifts — split it into three parts:

🛍️

SPEND

Use this for small things you want right now — a snack, a small toy, a book.

About 1/3 of your money

🐷

SAVE

Keep this for a bigger goal — something special you're working towards.

About 1/3 of your money

❤️

GIVE

Share a little with people who need help — a charity, a friend, or a food bank.

About 1/3 of your money

💡 Why Give?
Giving feels AMAZING. Science shows that when we help others, our brains release happy chemicals. You feel warm and proud. 🤩
Giving also reminds us that money isn't just about us — it can change someone's day!

📚 Source: CFPB — Money as You Grow & PBS Parents

🏆 Grand Final Quiz!

This is the BIG quiz — covering EVERYTHING you've learned! You've got this! 💪

Q1 — What are the THREE jars called?
✅ Perfect! Spend, Save, Give — the three magic jars! 🛍️🐷❤️
🙈 The three jars are: Spend, Save, Give! Say it out loud: Spend, Save, Give!
Q2 — Why do we have a GIVE jar?
✅ Yes! Giving helps others AND makes YOU feel wonderful! Kindness is powerful! ❤️
🙈 The Give jar is for sharing with people who need help — and it feels amazing to give!
Q3 — Zara gets 9 coins. She splits them equally into 3 jars. How many coins per jar?
✅ Brilliant maths! 9 ÷ 3 = 3 coins in each jar! You're so clever! 🧮🌟
🙈 9 ÷ 3 = 3 coins each! Divide the total coins by the number of jars!
Q4 — Which jar do you use for saving up to buy a bicycle?
✅ Correct! The SAVE jar is for big goals — like saving up for a bicycle! 🐷🚲
🙈 Use the SAVE jar for big goals! It's where your future dreams live! 🚲
Q5 — Money comes from ___
✅ Exactly right! Money comes from working — chores at home, or a job when you're grown up! 💪
🙈 Money comes from doing work and earning it — chores for you, jobs for grown-ups!
Q6 — A NEED is different from a WANT because:
✅ That's it! A need keeps you healthy and safe — without it, something bad could happen!
🙈 A need is something you must have to stay healthy and safe — like food, water, and shelter!
Q7 — True or False: A penny is worth MORE than a quarter.
✅ False! A quarter (25¢) is worth WAY more than a penny (1¢). Size doesn't mean more! 🌟
🙈 False! A quarter (25¢) is worth much more than a penny (1¢) — always check the number!
Q8 — What is the BEST thing to do with money you've just earned?
✅ You've learned everything! Split it up — a little to spend, some to save, some to give! CHAMPION! 🏆
🙈 The BEST move: split it — Spend some, Save some, Give some! Balance is the key!
