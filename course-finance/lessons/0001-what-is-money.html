What is Money?

Let's find out why money is so special!

📖 Story Time

Meet Zara 🐨 — a little koala who loves toys and yummy fruit. One day, Zara went to the toy shop and saw the most beautiful stuffed elephant. The shopkeeper said: "That will be five coins, please!"

Zara reached into her little pocket and pulled out... five shiny coins! 🪙🪙🪙🪙🪙 She handed them over, and walked home with her new elephant. 🐘

But what were those coins? And why did the shopkeeper want them? Let's find out!

🤔 So, What IS Money?

Money is something that everyone agrees is worth something.

Long, long ago, people didn't have money. If you wanted bread, you had to give the baker something he wanted — maybe some eggs, or a fish. This was called trading.

But what if the baker didn't want eggs that day? It was tricky! 😅

So people invented money — coins and notes that everyone agrees to accept. Now the baker always says "yes" to money!

💡 Money
Something special that you can swap for food, toys, or anything else you need or want.

🪙 What does money look like?

Money comes in two forms:

  • Coins — round and made of metal. They make a clink sound! 🎵
  • Bills (or notes) — flat and made of paper. They have pictures on them. 🖼️

Why does everyone want money?

Because you can use money to get almost anything — food, clothes, toys, a cozy home. That's why it's so useful!

📚 Source: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — Money as You Grow
