# Mission: Finance for My 5-Year-Old

## Why
A child who understands money basics early develops healthy lifelong habits — saving, making choices, and understanding value. The goal is to give a 5-year-old their first confident relationship with money: what it is, how it's earned, and why some of it should be saved.

## Success looks like
- Can identify coins and bills by name and rough value
- Understands that money is earned through work (chores/help)
- Can explain the difference between a **need** and a **want**
- Knows that saving means waiting to get something bigger later
- Can use a simple three-jar system: Spend, Save, Give
- Completes quizzes at the end of each lesson with ≥ 80% correct

## Constraints
- Learner is 5 years old — very short attention span (10–15 min per session)
- Language must be simple, concrete, and story-driven
- Activities must be visual and interactive (buttons, clicks, pictures)
- No abstract concepts (stocks, debt, compound interest) in this phase
- Parent/guardian may sit alongside during lessons

## Out of scope
- Banking, credit cards, digital payments
- Budgeting beyond the three-jar concept
- Earning outside the home (jobs, businesses)
- Taxes, inflation, investment
