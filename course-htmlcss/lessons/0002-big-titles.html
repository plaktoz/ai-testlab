🎯 What is a Heading?

A heading is a big title on your page — like the title on the cover of a book.

HTML has six sizes of headings. Today we'll learn the three biggest:

h1 — The BIGGEST title (your page's name!)
My Amazing Page
h2 — A section title
About Me
h3 — A smaller section title
My Favourite Things
💡 The number tells you the size. <h1> = biggest, <h6> = tiniest. Think of it like a ruler — 1 is at the top!

✏️ How to Write a Heading

Just like <b>, every heading tag has a start and an end:

<h1>This is my page!</h1> <h2>About Me</h2> <h3>My Favourite Colour</h3>
Rule of thumb: Use only one <h1> per page — it's the star of the show! You can use as many <h2> and <h3> tags as you like.

✏️ Try It — Update Your Page!

Open the mypage.html file you made in Lesson 1 and make it look like this:

<html> <body> <h1>My name is YOUR NAME!</h1> <h2>About Me</h2> Hello! My name is <b>YOUR NAME</b>! <h2>My Favourite Things</h2> I love ... </body> </html>
1
Replace YOUR NAME with your real name
2
Fill in what you love (animals? LEGO? pizza?)
3
Save and open the file in your browser — your name should be huge!
What should you see? Your name as a giant title, two smaller section titles, and your bold name in a sentence.

📚 What Did You Learn Today?

  • <h1> makes the biggest title — use it once per page
  • <h2> makes a medium title — great for sections
  • <h3> makes a smaller title — great for sub-sections
  • Lower number = larger text
