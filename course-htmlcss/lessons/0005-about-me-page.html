📋 The Full Code — Copy This!

Here is the complete code for your About Me page. We'll walk through it piece by piece below.

<!DOCTYPE html> <html> <head> <title>MY NAME's Page</title> </head> <body> <h1>My name is MY NAME! 👋</h1> <h2>About Me</h2> <p> Hello! My name is <strong>MY NAME</strong>. I am <strong>MY AGE</strong> years old. I love <em>exploring</em> new things! </p> <h2>My Favourite Things</h2> <ul> <li>FAVOURITE THING 1</li> <li>FAVOURITE THING 2</li> <li>FAVOURITE THING 3</li> </ul> <h2>My Favourite Colour</h2> <p>My favourite colour is <strong>COLOUR</strong>.</p> </body> </html>
New tag alert! <title> goes inside <head> and sets the name you see on the browser tab at the top. It doesn't appear on the page itself.
Also new: <!DOCTYPE html> on the very first line tells the browser "this is an HTML5 page." It has no closing tag — it's a one-line announcement!

🔍 Let's Walk Through It

1
<!DOCTYPE html> + <html> — the envelope for the whole page. Every HTML file ever made starts exactly like this.
2
<head><title>MY NAME</title></head> — secret info for the browser. Replace MY NAME so your tab shows YOUR name!
3
<h1> — your name in huge letters. The star of the show. Use it only once.
4
<h2> + <p> — a section title and a paragraph. Use <strong> inside the paragraph to highlight your name and age. Use <em> to add feeling.
5
<ul> + <li> — the shopping bag and its items! Add as many <li> lines as you like.
6
The last <h2> + <p> adds your favourite colour section. You can add more sections — a pet section, a food section, anything!

✏️ Build It — Step by Step

Create a brand new file called about-me.html on your Desktop. Then follow these steps:

1
Copy the full code from the purple box above into your new file
2
Replace every word written in CAPITALS with your own real information
3
Add at least 3 things to your favourite things list
4
Save the file, then double-click it to open in your browser
5
Look at the browser tab at the top — does it show your name? Look at the page — do you see your big h1 title? Your list?
Want to add more? Invent your own sections! Try <h2>My Pet</h2> or <h2>My Family</h2> and fill them in with <p> and <strong>. This is your page — make it as big as you like!
