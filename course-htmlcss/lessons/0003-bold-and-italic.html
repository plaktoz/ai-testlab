Bold & Italic 💪

Give your words personality — shout, whisper, and everything in between!

🔁 Quick Recap

You already know how to:

  • Build the skeleton of a page: <html>, <head>, <body>
  • Add big titles: <h1>, <h2>, <h3>
  • Make text bold with <b>
Haven't done Lessons 1 & 2 yet? Start at Lesson 1!

💪 <strong> — Super Important Words

You know <b> makes text bold. <strong> does the same thing — but it also tells the computer "this word really matters!"

🔊 Strong = like shouting something important
Watch out — the floor is very hot!

Here is how you write it:

<p>The most important thing is to have <strong>fun</strong>!</p>
Notice anything new? That's <p> — it stands for paragraph. It wraps a sentence or block of normal text. You'll use it a lot on your About Me page!

💬 <em> — Words Said with Feeling

<em> stands for emphasis. It tilts your text sideways — that's called italic. Use it when a word should be said in a special, expressive way.

🎭 Em = leaning in with feeling
I love pizza more than anything in the world.

Here is how you write it:

<p>My <em>favourite</em> colour is blue.</p>

✏️ Try It — Make Your Page More Exciting!

Open your mypage.html and update the body to look like this. Fill in the blanks with things you love!

<html> <body> <h1>My name is YOUR NAME!</h1> <h2>About Me</h2> <p>Hello! My name is <strong>YOUR NAME</strong>. I am <strong>5</strong> years old.</p> <h2>My Favourite Things</h2> <p>I <em>love</em> FAVOURITE THING. It makes me <em>so happy</em>!</p> </body> </html>
1
Replace YOUR NAME and FAVOURITE THING with your real answers
2
Save and open the file in your browser
3
You should see your name in bold and some words in italic — your page is getting a personality! 🎉

📚 What Did You Learn Today?

  • <strong> makes text bold and tells the computer it's important
  • <em> makes text italic — tilted and expressive
  • <p> wraps a normal paragraph of text
