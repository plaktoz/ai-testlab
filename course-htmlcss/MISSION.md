# Mission: HTML for Kids

## Why
A 5-year-old is learning to build his very first webpage. The goal is for him to experience the magic of making something appear in a browser with his own hands — building confidence and curiosity about how the web works.

## Success looks like
- He can create a new HTML file from scratch
- He can write a page about himself with his name as a big title
- He knows how to make text big (h1, h2), bold, and italic
- He can open his page in a browser and feel proud of what he made

## Constraints
- Learner is 5 years old — vocabulary must be extremely simple, no jargon
- Very short attention span — lessons must be completable in under 10 minutes
- Needs an adult nearby to help with typing and file saving
- No prior experience with computers, code, or HTML

## Out of scope
- CSS styling (colours, fonts) — keep to basic HTML elements only for now
- JavaScript or interactivity
- Publishing to the internet
- Forms, links, or tables
