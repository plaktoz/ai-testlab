# Notes

- Learner is 5 years old — all lesson language must be aimed at a child, not the parent
- An adult will be present to help with typing and saving files
- Keep lessons to a single concept with one small hands-on exercise
- Use big fonts, colour, and playful design in every lesson
- Avoid the word "code" — use "magic words" or "HTML words" instead
- Relate everything to things a 5-year-old knows: favourite colour, their name, animals, toys
